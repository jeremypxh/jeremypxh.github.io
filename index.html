SCOUT: Spatiotemporal Coverage for Optimal Unmanned Tasking

Demand for UAVs is rarely uniform: in the January 2025 Los Angeles wildfires, fire intensity varied sharply across areas and over time. SCOUT first places UAVs in high-demand areas, then refines their positions with an iterative, gradient-based update that minimizes a cost combining spatial distance and demand density. It outperforms 3D K-means and weighted Voronoi on both accessibility and equity.

  • UAV deployment
  • Coverage control
  • Heterogeneous demand

Manufacturing Letters 2026 · NAMRC 54First author

STEM: STructural EMbedding for Graph Representation of 3D Designs

STEM encodes the self-organizing graph of a 3D design into a compact embedding for matching and retrieval. A dual-channel encoder combines graph attention (local structure) with an MLP (global structure), and a hierarchical loss keeps the embedding robust to rotation and node relabeling. It outperforms random forest, softmax, SVM and XGBoost baselines on both test datasets, reaching 92.9% main-category accuracy on the larger one.

  • Graph neural networks
  • 3D design retrieval
  • Manufacturing analytics

Ongoing · Penn StateFirst author

Adaptive Inverse DOE for Online Calibration of Cardiac Electrical Signaling

Biophysical models of heart cells serve as digital twins but are usually calibrated offline with fixed patch-clamp protocols. This framework keeps a digital twin synchronized with a physical cardiomyocyte: a revised Adam optimizer updates parameters after every protocol, and an adaptive inverse design of experiments adds and reconfigures protocols. In simulation studies it recovers ion-channel parameters more accurately than fixed-protocol, Adam and Bayesian baselines.

  • Digital twins
  • Design of experiments
  • Cardiac electrophysiology

SJTU · HKUST · 2021–2024

Wind Power Grid Integration: From Ramp Prediction to Economic Dispatch

Wind power ramps disrupt grid dispatch. This project predicts ramp events with WP-GAT, a graph attention network on a spatio-temporal wind propagation graph, and generates multi-farm wind power scenarios with spatiotemporal covariance functions and copulas. The scenarios are tested in two-stage dispatch models, one solved with deep reinforcement learning, and a multi-objective model uses demand response to absorb ramp events.

  • Graph attention networks
  • Scenario generation
  • Economic dispatch

SJTU · 2021

Integrated Berth and Quay-Crane Allocation at Yangshan Port Phase IV

At Shanghai's automated Yangshan Port Phase IV terminal, berth and quay-crane plans were made separately, by hand and over short horizons. This project builds a mixed-integer model that allocates both together under Phase IV operating rules, solved with Gurobi, and designs particle swarm and differential evolution heuristics that cut solve time by about 95% on a small test case.

  • Mixed-integer programming
  • Metaheuristics
  • Port logistics
