SCOUT: Spatiotemporal Coverage for Optimal Unmanned Tasking

Where should a drone swarm be when the demand it serves keeps moving? SCOUT plans UAV coverage for spatiotemporal demand, such as a spreading wildfire. It places drones one at a time, then refines the whole swarm by moving each drone toward the demand-weighted centroid of the area it covers.

  • UAV swarms
  • Coverage optimization
  • Wildfire monitoring

Framework figure · coming soon
Manufacturing Letters 2026 · NAMRC 54First author

STEM: STructural EMbedding for Graph Representation of 3D Designs

STEM represents 3D designs as graphs and learns embeddings of their structure, giving machine-learning models a compact, structure-aware view of design data for manufacturing.

  • Graph learning
  • 3D design
  • Manufacturing

Renewable Energy 2024 · 2022

Spatiotemporal learning for wind power

A graph attention network on a spatio-temporal wind propagation graph that predicts wind power ramp events, plus spatio-temporal dependency models that generate wind power scenarios across multiple farms for grid dispatch.

  • Forecasting
  • Energy systems
