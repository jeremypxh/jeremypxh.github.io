Shanghai Jiao Tong University · 2021

Integrated Berth and Quay-Crane Allocation at Yangshan Port Phase IV

Xinghao Peng · advised by Prof. Wei Qin

School of Mechanical Engineering · Shanghai Jiao Tong University

01Challenge

At Shanghai’s automated Yangshan Port Phase IV terminal, berth and quay-crane plans decide how long vessels stay at berth and how efficiently the whole terminal runs.

02Gap

Plans covered short time spans, allocated berths and quay cranes separately and were made by hand. Solving the joint problem exactly also takes too long at realistic sizes: Gurobi needed about 11 days for an instance with 22 vessels.

03Our contribution

  1. Integrated model. A mixed-integer program allocates berths and quay cranes together under Phase IV operating rules, minimizing vessels’ total time at berth first and quay-crane movements second.
  2. Longer horizons. Solving consecutive periods jointly gives shorter total time at berth than solving them one by one, which motivates a rolling time-window scheme.
  3. Fast heuristics. Particle swarm optimization and differential evolution, with a whole-plan encoding and adaptive parameters, cut solve time by 94–95% on a small instance while staying within 6–9% of the exact solution.
